    About the Community Corner

    The Community Corner is a free weekly paper for the Jewish community in Pomona, Monsey, Wesley Hills and Montebello. Every week it brings a story, divrei Torah, an interview, games, a recipe and more, written by people from our own community. It is printed and dropped off at local shuls, and every issue is here online.

    Editor-in-chief: Doni Stern
